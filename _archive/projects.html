I currently work with Prof. Aninda Sinha on strongly coupled quantum field theories using tensor networks and quantum computing. Previously at IISc, I was fortunate to contribute to several projects spanning quantum information, particle physics, and field theory.

  • Entanglement in Yukawa–SYK. I am currently studying entanglement dynamics and pure-state evolution in the Yukawa–SYK model.
  • Quantum computing for quantum field theory (Master’s thesis). I worked on photonic quantum computing and developed a simulation workflow building on StrawberryFields, with applications to scattering in one- and two-dimensional quantum mechanics. My current efforts are to extend this package to study QFTs.
  • Black holes and dark matter. With Prof. Nirmal Raj and Dr. Anupam Ray, I studied the memory-burden mechanism as an alternative perspective on the black-hole information problem. We showed that memory burden can significantly strengthen observational constraints on dark-matter and neutron scattering cross sections.
  • $\epsilon$-expansion via Polyakov–Mellin bootstrap (Bachelor’s thesis). I studied dispersion relations in QFT/CFT and used non-perturbative bootstrap techniques to investigate the $\epsilon$-expansion.
  • Lattice gauge theory and matrix quantum mechanics (Summer 2022). With Prof. Anosh Joseph, I studied the bosonic sector of the mini-BMN matrix model, an $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ matrix quantum mechanics. This was my first serious research project and sparked my interest in quantum field theory.

About me

Hi, I am Ujjwal. I study theoretical physics. I have previously worked on critical phenomena and the conformal bootstrap, quantum computing and tensor networks, and entanglement in SYK models. Some of these projects have led to publications. This page is a collection of my projects.

Apart from physics, I am interested in art, programming, math and animal welfare. My résumé is available here.

About me

Hi! My name is Ujjwal. I am interested in almost the entirety of theoretical physics, but if I had to pick something in particular, it would be the subject of Quantum Field Theory. A large part of my time therefore is spent studying QFT, primarily from a high energy theory perspective, but I am also interested in it from a mathematical physics and condensed matter theory viewpoint. I am also interested in particle phenomenology.

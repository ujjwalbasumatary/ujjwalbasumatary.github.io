Lectures on QFT on a QC

[2512.02706] Lectures on Quantum Field Theory on a Quantum Computer — Aninda Sinha and Ujjwal Basumatary.

These notes grew out of HE381 at IISc, taught by Prof. Aninda Sinha in the fall of 2025, with me as the teaching assistant. They introduce quantum computing and tensor-network methods through the anharmonic oscillator, Ising field theory, the Schwinger model, and lattice φ4 theory. They are currently under review at Texts and Readings in Physical Sciences (TRiPS), co-published by Springer Nature and Hindustan Book Agency.

The accompanying codebase contains Python and Qiskit teaching notebooks, Julia simulations, and student reports and presentations. I was responsible for implementing the algorithms and currently maintain the repository.

You can find the Hamiltonians, setup instructions, and commands for running the calculations in the documentation.

Lectures on QFT on a QC

[2512.02706] Lectures on Quantum Field Theory on a Quantum Computer — Aninda Sinha and Ujjwal Basumatary.

These notes grew out of HE381 at IISc, taught by Prof. Aninda Sinha in the fall of 2025, with Ujjwal Basumatary as the teaching assistant. They introduce quantum computing and tensor-network methods through the anharmonic oscillator, Ising field theory, the Schwinger model, and lattice φ4 theory. They are currently under review at Hindustan Book Agency.

The accompanying codebase contains Python and Qiskit teaching notebooks, Julia simulations, and student reports and presentations. Ujjwal implemented the scripts and maintains the repository.

The Julia scripts use matrix product states to represent the quantum state.

  • collide_fixed_momentum.jl implements the time evolution of two colliding wave packets in Ising field theory. It records the changes in the energy on each bond and the spin expectation value at each site relative to the vacuum.
  • collide_wavepackets.jl implements the time evolution of two colliding wave packets in lattice φ4 theory. Each lattice site is represented in a truncated oscillator basis.
  • source_quench.jl implements a source quench in the bosonized Schwinger model. It first finds the ground state with a source on the central sites, then changes the source strength and calculates the field expectation value at each site as a function of time.

You can find the Hamiltonians, setup instructions, and commands for running the calculations in the documentation.
